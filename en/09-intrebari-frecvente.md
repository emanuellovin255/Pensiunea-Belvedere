## How is a booking confirmed?

A booking is confirmed once a deposit of 30% of the chosen package has been paid. The deposit is
transferred to the company account and is non-refundable, but it can be rescheduled for genuine
reasons if you let us know at least 3 weeks in advance.

## Do you accept Romanian holiday vouchers?

Yes, in any form. Paper vouchers can be sent by courier as the deposit that confirms a booking.
Electronic holiday cards are accepted, but bookings of this kind are made strictly at reception —
bookings that come through Booking.com and are paid by holiday card cannot be settled.

## Are there discounts for children?

Yes, on the holiday packages: 75% for children aged 2–4, 50% for 4–8 and 25% for 8–12.

## What if I don't eat fish?

Tell us when you book and we will replace the fish dishes with meat dishes, at no extra cost. On
the Easter package, guests who don't eat lamb can have pork or chicken instead.

## How long is the wait at the restaurant?

Between 30 and 35 minutes. Dishes are cooked on the spot and to order, never kept ready made —
that is why it takes time. Fish soup is made by pre-order only, for a minimum of 4 people.

## Where do the Delta trips leave from?

From the port of Murighiol, on large covered boats. There are six routes, between 2.5 hours and a
full day, and at Mila 23, the Letea Forest and the Caraorman Forest you can have a meal at a local
food stop, for an extra charge.

## How do I get to the guesthouse, and where do I park?

You can drive right up to the gate. From Bucharest it is a drive of roughly 4 hours. Parking is
inside the courtyard, free and with no booking needed, and for electric cars we have a charging
point.

## How do I get into my room?

Rooms are opened with a key card. The grounds are covered by video surveillance, and every room
has its own safe and smoke detectors.

## Are the pool and the playground charged separately?

No. The heated pool, the play complex, the football pitch and the fishing pontoon are included in
the price, but access is reserved for guests staying with us. People who are not booked into the
guesthouse cannot use these areas, not even for a fee.

## Can the whole guesthouse be booked?

Yes. We host team-building events and rent out the entire place for private events, with tailored
packages covering meals, accommodation and boat trips, depending on what you need. Send us a
request and we will come back with an offer.
