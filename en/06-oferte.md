Text introductiv: Two packages with meals and a day-by-day programme, plus six boat routes leaving from the port of Murighiol. Trips are booked at reception, depending on the weather and the number of people.

## Promotional package, 3 nights and 4 days

Poza: exterior-pensiune-piscina.webp
Badge: Most requested
Valabil: May – September
Include:
Three nights' accommodation, breakfast every morning
Day 1 — a fisherman's platter from 16:00, then roast carp with cabbage salad and dessert from 19:30
Day 2 — an 8-hour trip into the Delta, choosing between Sulina, Mila 23, the Letea Forest or Sfântu Gheorghe; in the evening, carp saramură with polenta and dessert
Day 3 — a 2.5-hour trip across seven lakes; lunch with storceag; in the evening, fish balls with mashed potatoes, salad and dessert
Day 4 — check-out by 12:00
Free access to the heated pool, the playground, the football pitch and the fishing pontoon
Discounts for children — 75% for ages 2–4, 50% for 4–8, 25% for 8–12
Meals during the trips and the safari are not included in the price — they are optional

The price is per person and differs from month to month: May 1,500 lei, June 1,600 lei, July
1,700 lei, August 1,800 lei, September 1,600 lei. Drinks are not included. Lunch during the
full-day trip is paid separately, at the local food stop. The package does not apply to single
occupancy. For guests who don't eat fish, the dishes are replaced with meat, on prior request.
Lunch and the safari are optional and are not included in the price.

## Easter package 2026

Poza: restaurant-sala.webp
Badge: 10–13 April 2026
Valabil: 10–13 April 2026, 4 days / 3 nights
Include:
Three nights' accommodation, breakfast every morning, plus an Easter breakfast on the third day
Day 1 — check-in from 12:00; lunch with a fisherman's platter; dinner of oven-roasted fish with potatoes and cabbage salad, with Dobrogea pie for dessert
Day 2 — an optional boat trip; lunch with catfish storceag and fried fish with polenta; dinner of carp saramură and papanași with jam
Day 3 — Easter breakfast with painted eggs and traditional dishes; lunch with soup and roast lamb, pickled pepper and cucumber salad; dinner of fish balls with mashed potatoes and sauce, roasted pepper salad and home-made cakes
Day 4 — breakfast and check-out by 12:00
Free access to the heated pool and the leisure areas
Discounts for children — 75% for ages 2–4, 50% for 4–8, 25% for 8–12

The package is 1,400 lei per person. Guests can attend the Easter service at the village church.
Drinks are not included. For guests who don't eat lamb, it can be replaced with pork or chicken, on
prior request. The package does not apply to single occupancy. Payment can also be made with a
Romanian holiday voucher.

## Boat trip: Sulina beach

Poza: excursie-plaja-sulina.webp
Valabil: about 2.5 hours

The boat leaves the port of Murighiol along the Sfântu Gheorghe branch — the Old Danube — the
village of Uzlina — the Uzlina channel — Lake Uzlina, where you see the water lilies, the birds and
the wildlife of the Delta — Lake Durnuleapca — Lake Isăcel — Lake Isac — the Isac channel — the
Litcov channel — the Lung channel, which comes out into the Sulina branch — the village of Crișan —
the Danube — Sulina beach, the final destination.

## Boat trip: Sfântu Gheorghe beach

Poza: excursie-plaja-sf-gheorghe.webp
Valabil: about 2.5 hours

The boat leaves the port of Murighiol along the Sfântu Gheorghe branch — the Old Danube — the
village of Uzlina — the Uzlina channel — Lake Uzlina, with the water lilies and birds of the Delta —
Lake Durnuleapca — Lake Isăcel — Lake Isac — the Isac channel — the Litcov channel — the
Caraorman–Crișan channel — the Caraorman channel — Lake Puiu — the Mocansca channel — Lake
Erenciuc, which comes out onto the Danube, heading for Sfântu Gheorghe.

## Boat trip: the Letea Forest

Poza: excursie-padurea-letea.webp
Valabil: about 2.5 hours to the destination

The boat leaves the port of Murighiol towards the Sulina branch — the village of Crișan — the Old
Danube — the Magiaru channel — the Letea channel — the village of Letea. From there you go on
safari by horse cart or small off-road vehicles to visit the Letea Forest, and afterwards you can
have a meal with a local family, at a food stop.

## Boat trip: the Caraorman Forest

Poza: excursie-padurea-caraorman.webp
Valabil: about 2.5 hours

The boat leaves the port of Murighiol along the Sfântu Gheorghe branch — the Old Danube — the
village of Uzlina — the Uzlina channel — Lake Uzlina, with the water lilies and birds of the Delta —
Lake Durnuleapca — Lake Isăcel — Lake Isac — the Isac channel — the Litcov channel — the
Caraorman–Crișan channel — the Caraorman channel — the village of Caraorman. From there you can go
on safari to visit the forest in special vehicles, and have a meal with a local family, at a food
stop.

## Boat trip: seven lakes of the Delta

Poza: excursie-sase-lacuri.webp
Valabil: 2.5 – 3 hours

The short run, to take in the wildlife, the birds and the flora of the Danube Delta, across the
lakes Uzlina, Durnuleapca, Isăcel, CabluVata, Criscioara and Hleboca. This is the route to pick if
you have small children or only half a day free.

## Boat trip: Mila 23

Poza: excursie-mila-23.webp
Valabil: about 2 hours each way

The boat goes to the village of Mila 23, where you visit the Ivan Patzaichin Museum. After the
visit you can have a meal, for an extra charge, at a food stop we work with.
